Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Who we are

Flyaith (“we”, “us”) provides an AI-assisted travel planning mobile app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email address, name, and authentication identifiers when you sign in (including Google sign-in).
  • Onboarding preferences — favorite creators/influencers, destinations, and similar taste signals you choose.
  • Trip & chat content — messages, trip briefs, selected flights/places, YouTube links you paste, and saved past trips.
  • Location (optional) — approximate or precise location only if you grant permission, used to show your position on trip maps.
  • Device notifications — push notification tokens if you enable trip reminders.
  • Diagnostics — basic technical logs needed to keep the service reliable and secure.

How we use information

  • Create and personalize travel plans (including AI-generated itineraries).
  • Search flights, places, and related travel data via third-party providers.
  • Save and restore your conversations / past trips.
  • Authenticate your account and prevent abuse.
  • Send optional trip reminders you enable.
  • Improve product quality and fix bugs.

AI processing

Trip planning uses third-party AI model providers to generate replies and itineraries from your chat and trip context. Do not include sensitive personal data you do not want processed for planning.

Third-party services

Depending on features you use, data may be processed by:

  • Authentication / database hosting (e.g. Supabase)
  • AI model providers (e.g. Google Gemini)
  • Travel search providers (e.g. SerpAPI / Google results partners)
  • Sign-in providers (e.g. Google)
  • Apple services required to distribute and operate the iOS app

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data retention

We keep account and trip data while your account is active so you can resume past plans. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting support.

Your choices

  • You can deny location or notification permissions in system settings.
  • You can sign out and stop using the app at any time.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by emailing support.

Children

Flyaith is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@flyaith.app or open an issue on GitHub. Also see our Support page.
