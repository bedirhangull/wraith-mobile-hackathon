Common topics

  • Sign-in — try again with Google or email; make sure you’re online.
  • Trip chat — paste a YouTube travel link, pick a creator route, or plan freeform in chat.
  • Past trips — open Profile → Past Trips to resume a saved conversation.
  • Permissions — location and notifications are optional; change them anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Subscriptions — manage or cancel Flyaith Premium in your Apple ID subscriptions.
